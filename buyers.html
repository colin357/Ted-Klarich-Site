First-time buyers

Nobody is born knowing what "escrow" means.

First-time buyers get talked down to constantly — handed jargon, then made to feel behind for asking what it means. That's backwards. You've never done this before. Of course you have questions.

We start with the questions underneath the questions: what your monthly payment would actually look like, what you'd need saved, what your credit does and doesn't allow, and whether buying now or in eight months puts you in a stronger position.

The goal isn't to get you approved. It's to get you approved for something you'll still feel good about in year three.

VA loans

You earned this benefit. Let's use it properly.

The VA loan is one of the strongest financing tools in the country — and one of the most misunderstood, sometimes by the agents on the other side of your offer. Half my job here is making sure your offer is taken as seriously as it deserves to be.

Entitlement

How much of your benefit is available, what happens if you've used it before, and how to restore it. Most veterans have more room than they think.

Zero down

Eligible buyers can finance up to the full purchase price. We'll still talk through whether putting money down serves you better.

The funding fee

What it costs, when it's financed, and who's exempt — including service-connected disability exemptions people are often never told about.

Offer strength

A VA offer competes fine when the financing is explained properly. I call the listing agent myself and make sure it is.

Eligibility and terms depend on your service history and current VA guidelines. Bring me your situation and we'll confirm exactly what's available to you.

FHA

FHA loans

A lower barrier in — with an exit planned from day one.

FHA financing allows a lower down payment and more flexible credit guidelines than conventional loans, which makes it a real door-opener for a lot of buyers. It also carries mortgage insurance that behaves differently than conventional PMI. We'll compare both side by side with actual numbers, and if FHA is the right move, we'll talk about what would eventually let you refinance out of it.

INV

Investment properties

Financing built around the return, not just the purchase.

Rental purchases live or die on structure: down payment requirements, reserves, how rental income is counted, and how this property affects the next one you want to buy. Whether it's your first duplex or your sixth door, we'll look at the financing as part of a portfolio rather than a one-off transaction — because the loan you take today decides what you can do in eighteen months.

REL

Relocation

Two closings, one moving truck, zero margin for error.

Relocating means a new job start date, a lease or a home sale on the other end, and a timeline where one slipped deadline costs you real money. This is exactly the kind of file where anticipating problems matters most: coordinating the sale and the purchase, handling income from a job you haven't started yet, and building in the buffer nobody thinks about until they need it.

Something that doesn't fit a box

Self-employed, new to the country, complicated income, unusual property.

Not every good borrower looks tidy on paper. Self-employment, bonus and commission income, recent job changes, or a property type that makes a lender nervous — these files need someone willing to actually work them. That's the part of this job I like most. Tell me what's complicated and let's see what's possible.
