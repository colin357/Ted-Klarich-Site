Why people call me

Getting a mortgage shouldn't feel like being handed a form and wished good luck.

Most of this industry is built to process loans. Application link, rate quote, automated status email, done. Efficient — right up until you have an actual question.

But a mortgage isn't a form. It's the biggest financial decision most people ever make, usually during one of the most stressful months of their life, and usually with a deadline attached.

So I built my business the other way around: understand the person first, then build the loan around them. That means longer first conversations, more explaining, and more of my own effort and energy poured into files that a bare-minimum lender would just push through.

It's more work. That's the point.

"Can we actually afford this?"
"Should we buy now or keep waiting?"
"Is my VA benefit the right move here?"
"What happens after I send the documents?"
The TED Standard

Three words end this process well. All three of them end in TED.

It started as a joke about my name. It turned into the checklist I run every loan against — because if a client can't say all three of these at closing, I didn't do my job.

01

TrusTED

"I believe what he told me."

Straight answers, including the ones that cost me the deal. If waiting six months is the smarter move, I'll say so. Advice you can't trust is worse than no advice at all.

02

DedicaTED

"He worked harder than he had to."

Extra calls to the listing agent. Structuring the file three ways to find the one that fits. Answering the text at 8pm on a Sunday because that's when the offer is due.

03

AppreciaTED

"I felt like a person, not a file number."

You'll be updated before you have to ask, thanked more than once, and remembered long after the keys change hands. Clients aren't leads. They're the whole business.

The communication promise

You should never have to wonder what's happening with your loan.

Every lender claims they communicate well. Here's the difference between claiming it and doing it.

The update most people get

"Your loan is still in process. We'll let you know."

The update I send

"Appraisal came back at value — no issues there. Underwriting has the file and asked for two things: your June bank statement and a quick letter about the deposit on the 12th. Send those today and we stay on track for Friday's closing. I'll confirm the minute they're cleared."

Specific. Proactive. Sent before you had to chase it. If you ever find yourself wondering where things stand, call me — but you shouldn't have to.

About Ted

Hi, I'm Ted. I do the parts of this job most people skip.

I'm a mortgage loan officer with Fairway Home Mortgage, based in Washington, and I work with people across the state at every stage of homeownership — first-time buyers figuring out whether they're even ready, veterans using a benefit they've earned, homeowners refinancing or right-sizing, and investors adding to a portfolio.

What ties those together isn't the loan type. It's how the process feels. I want you to leave every conversation knowing more than you did going in, and I'd rather over-explain than let you sign something you don't fully understand.

TrusTED. DedicaTED. AppreciaTED. Turns out a lot of good words end in TED.

The other half of my job is the part clients don't see: chasing down the underwriter, calling the listing agent to back up your offer, and finding the structure that saves you money instead of the one that's fastest for me. That's the extra effort and energy I bring — and honestly, it's the only way I know how to do this.

What working together looks like

Six steps. You'll always know which one you're on.

STEP 01

The first conversation

Twenty minutes about your goals, timeline and worries. No application, no credit pull, no pressure. Just an honest read on where you stand.

STEP 02

Your strategy

Down payment, loan type, timing and monthly payment laid out side by side in plain English — including what each option actually costs you.

STEP 03

Pre-approval

Documents gathered and reviewed up front, so your pre-approval is one an agent can hand a listing agent with confidence.

STEP 04

Offer support

When you find the house, I'm on the phone with your agent and the listing side the same day, making sure your financing strengthens your offer.

STEP 05

Underwriting to clear-to-close

Appraisal, conditions, deadlines. You'll hear from me at every milestone, and you'll hear about problems from me first — with a plan attached.

STEP 06

After closing

An annual check on your rate and equity, and a phone I still answer when life changes. Closing is the start of the relationship, not the end of it.
