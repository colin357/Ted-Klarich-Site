Why people call me

Buying a home is a big deal. You should not have to figure it out on your own.

Most lenders send you a link and a rate. Then they send emails from a computer. It works fine until you have a real question. Then you are stuck.

A home loan is the biggest money choice most people ever make. It comes with a deadline. It comes during a very busy month. You deserve a real person on the phone.

So I do it the other way. I get to know you first. Then I build the loan to fit you.

That takes longer. I like it that way.

"Should we buy now or keep waiting?"
"Is my VA benefit the right move here?"

The communication promise

You should never have to guess how your loan is going.

Every lender says they are good at keeping you posted. Look at the two updates below. That is the difference.

The update most people get

"Your loan is still in process. We'll let you know."

The update I send

"Good news. The home appraised for the full price, so we are fine there. The loan team needs two more things from you: your June bank statement, and a short note about the money you put in the bank on the 12th. Send those today and we still close on Friday. I will text you the second they clear."

You get real news, in plain words, before you have to ask. You can always call me. I just don't want you to need to.

What working together looks like

Six steps. You will always know which one you are on.

STEP 01

We talk

Twenty minutes. What you want, when you want it, what worries you. No forms. No credit check. Just an honest answer about where you stand.

STEP 02

We make a plan

I show you your choices side by side. How much money down. What kind of loan. What you would pay each month. And what each choice really costs you.

STEP 03

You get pre-approved

I collect your papers and check them early. That way your pre-approval letter holds up when it counts.

STEP 04

You make an offer

You find the house. That same day I call your agent and the seller's agent. I make sure your loan makes your offer look strong.

STEP 05

The loan gets checked

The home gets appraised. The loan team reviews your file. You hear from me at every step. If something goes wrong, you hear it from me first, with a plan.

STEP 06

You get the keys

Once a year I check your rate for you. And I still pick up the phone when life changes. Closing day is the start, not the end.
